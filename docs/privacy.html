Privacy Policy — Visual Message

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Visual Message ("the app") is designed to display short messages visually, as large on-screen characters or as Morse code light signals. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little data as possible.

Data the app collects

The app does not require an account and does not ask for any personal information. Your message text, saved messages, and settings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or to any server we control.

Camera

The optional Receive feature uses your device camera to detect flashing Morse code from another light source. Camera frames are processed on your device only to measure brightness; no images or video are recorded, stored, or sent to us or to any third party.

Advertising

The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, and approximate location. This processing is performed by Google, not by us. For details on how Google uses this information, see Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The app does not use any separate analytics or tracking services beyond the advertising described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at atte.tenkanen@gmail.com.
